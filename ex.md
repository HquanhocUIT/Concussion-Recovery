# Experience

1. **Choose the right problem:** Solve a specific, meaningful, real-world health problem—not just a cool technology.
2. **Go deep, not broad:** One strong core workflow is better than many shallow features.
3. **Make AI meaningful:** AI should provide real value, not just be a chatbot wrapper.
4. **Build Responsible AI:** Include safety, explainability, privacy, and human-in-the-loop mechanisms.
5. **Use evidence:** For healthcare projects, important features should be supported by research and evidence-based guidelines.
6. **Show real technical depth:** Focus on strong architecture and meaningful AI/ML pipelines rather than using many technologies just for show.
7. **Prioritize UX:** Make the product intuitive, accessible, and easy to use.
8. **Master the demo:** In 4 minutes, clearly show **Problem → Solution → AI → Impact → Safety**, and leave judges with one memorable differentiator.

> **Winning Formula:**
> 
> 
> **Real Problem + Unique Solution + Meaningful AI + Safety + Strong Engineering + Great UX + Killer Demo = Winning Hackathon Projec**
